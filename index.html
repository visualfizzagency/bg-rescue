How it works

  1. 1. Add photosDrag, browse or paste. Up to 20 at a time, any size.
  2. 2. AI cuts the backgroundBiRefNet (MIT-licensed) runs 100% in your browser — nothing is uploaded.
  3. 3. Download PNGsFull-resolution transparent PNG, one by one or all as a .zip.

Why is it free?

There is no server to pay for. The AI model downloads once to your browser (about 94–183 MB, cached afterwards) and every photo is processed on your own device. No accounts, no credits, no watermarks, no catch. BG Rescue is open source (MIT) — inspect the code on GitHub.

Privacy

Your photos never leave your device. The only download is the AI model itself, from Hugging Face. No analytics, no tracking, no cookies.
